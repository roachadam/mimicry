## Implemented

- **CORS check on redirects** for every CORS-mode request (`kinds.go`
  `corsOrigin`, `redirect.go` `refusedRedirect`): XHR, elements with
  `crossorigin`, fonts, besides fetch(). Credentials need ACAC.
- **`redirects.navigation_limit`** (Chrome 19, others 20; `redirect.go`
  `browserHop`). Without a `redirects` section a navigation's 20th
  redirect is `ErrNotMeasured`.
- **`redirects.hop_preflight`** (`own` Chrome, `inherit` Firefox/Safari;
  `preflight.go` `hopPreflight`): a hop of a preflighted request is
  preflighted even when simple. A forced preflight is only spared by a live
  cache entry for its URL.
- **Variant conditions**: `method_changed_by_redirect`,
  `insecure_before_redirect`, `site: none` (typed navigations); preflight
  `page_set` = asks for headers (Safari's order differs).
- **Safari's body without `content-length`** after a preflight
  (`headerorder.Plan.NoContentLength`, HTTP/2).
- **Activation**: a navigation hop whose request before sent
  `Sec-Fetch-User` needs a variant that places it, else `ErrNotMeasured`.
- **kindsgen** reads `redirects2` (bases `redir-<kind>`; Chrome's reload
  dropped) and every `nav-redirect-*` capture (typed chains, plain-HTTP
  first legs); navigation variant values come from the requests that send
  the header. All four profiles regenerated.
- Tests: `TestRedirects2MatchesBrowser`, `TestNavRedirectsMatchBrowser`
  (e2e); `TestNavigationRedirectLimit`, `TestRedirectCORSCheckBeyondFetch`,
  `TestHopPreflight`, `TestNavigationRedirectMeasured`,
  `TestRedirectHopConditions` (root); `TestCollectRedirects2`,
  `TestCollectNavRedirects` (kindsgen); validation cases (profile).

## Still `ErrNotMeasured` (navigation hops)

- A page's navigation (link or script) redirected to another origin
  (same-site or cross-site), GET or POST.
- In Chrome and Firefox, a link's navigation (sends `Sec-Fetch-User`)
  redirected within its origin: the captured page chain is a script's.
  Suppress `Sec-Fetch-User` (nil header) for a script's navigation.
- A typed navigation's second hop and later when one changes origin or
  scheme differently than captured (each hop's own condition must exist).
- Frames keep the fetch limit of 20 (not measured).

## Gaps the e2e tests list

- Connection pool not partitioned by top-level site (navigations to another
  site are compared apart from their connections).
- Firefox: `www.redir-c.test` hops credentialed / credential-less on two
  connections; markup images at `u=4` here (`u=5` on the kinds page).
- Chrome: a markup image's hop raised to `u=1` after layout.
- Chrome's error-page auto-reload is not reproduced.
