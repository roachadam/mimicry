# Cookies in Chrome 155, Firefox 158 and Safari 27, 2026-10-24

Which cookies each browser accepted, and which it sent on every request of
the kinds page (`kinds` and `kinds-http1` captures, 3 samples each, stable
unless noted). Input for `mimicry/cookies` (step 9.3).

## Set by the page (`www.kinds-a.test`, all `Secure`)

| Cookie | Chrome | Firefox | Safari |
|---|---|---|---|
| `k_strict`, `k_lax`, `k_none`, `k_unset`, `__Secure-k_ok` | kept | kept | kept |
| `k_site` (`Domain=kinds-a.test`) | kept; sent to `static.kinds-a.test` too | same | same |
| `k_quoted="Ab+/cd=="`, `k_space=a b c`, `k_comma=a,b`, `k_bslash=back\slash`, `k_json={"a":1}`, `k_empty=` | byte for byte | byte for byte | byte for byte |
| `k_utf=café` | byte for byte | byte for byte | **`cafÃ©`**: the UTF-8 bytes read as Latin-1 and re-encoded |
| `knoeq` (no `=`) | sent as `knoeq` | sent as `knoeq=` | **dropped** |
| `k_big` (4,100-byte value) | rejected | rejected | rejected |
| `k_dommis` (`Domain=other-site.test`) | rejected | rejected | rejected |
| `__Host-k_bad` (has `Domain`) | rejected | rejected | rejected |
| `k_expired` (past `Expires`), `k_maxage0` (`Max-Age=0`) | not stored | not stored | not stored |

## Third-party cookies (set by `cdn.kinds-b.test` on an image)

| Cookie | Chrome | Firefox | Safari |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kb_none` (`SameSite=None`) | sent cross-site | sent | **blocked** |
| `kb_unset` (no SameSite) | not sent (Lax by default) | **sent** (None by default) | blocked |
| `kb_lax` | not sent | not sent | blocked |
| `kb_part` (`Partitioned`, None) | sent | sent | sent (the only one) |

## When cookies are sent

All three agree with the credentials rule mimicry already computes: CORS
requests without credentials (cross-origin `fetch()` and XHR by default,
`crossorigin=anonymous` elements, `credentials: "omit"`) send none; no-cors
requests, frames, beacons and `credentials: "include"` send them. Safari's
fonts load no-cors, so its cross-site fonts carry `kb_part` (once varying:
`font-css.cs` sent it in some samples only).

Redirect back to the page's site (`fetch-redirect.cs-so.hop`,
`image-redirect.cs-so.hop`):

| Chrome | Firefox | Safari |
|---|---|---|
| every first-party cookie | all but `k_lax` and `k_strict`: SameSite judged on the whole chain | **none at all**, `SameSite=None` included (tracking prevention, [inference]) |

## Order

- Chrome and Firefox: creation order (every cookie here has path `/`).
- Safari: `k_site` (the domain cookie) first, then the host cookies by
  name. The `set-cookie` scenario, where every cookie shares a domain, only
  showed "by name". Whether Safari sorts by domain then name, or by
  specificity, needs mixed domains and paths.

## Not answered by these captures

- Lax and Strict on a cross-site top-level navigation (GET, form POST);
  Chrome's two-minute exception for new no-SameSite cookies on cross-site
  POSTs.
- Partition isolation: whether `kb_part` is withheld under another
  top-level site.
- `SameSite=None` without `Secure`.
- Order with mixed domains and paths.
- Whether Safari's redirect rule is about the redirect or these hosts.
